2. The law this notice is built on

Namibia does not currently have a general data protection Act in force. What protects your information, and what we are bound by, is Article 13 of the Namibian Constitution (your privacy), the Microlending Act 7 of 2018 (we may not disclose confidential information without your express consent, except to a registered credit bureau), the Credit Bureau Regulations, 2014, and the consent you give when you apply. We apply the standards in this notice as our own policy. If Namibia passes a data protection Act, we will update this notice.

4. Copies only, never originals

We never take, hold or use your bank card, your PIN, any banking password or any original document, not as security and not to collect a debt. This is prohibited by section 23(1)(f) of the Microlending Act. If anyone claiming to be from QuickServe asks you for them, refuse and report it to us and to NAMFISA.

5. Who we share it with

  • Registered credit bureaux we use, if any: your identity and how your account performs, both good and bad.
  • Your employer: only to confirm that you work there and that your payslip is genuine. We never discuss your loan with them.
  • Our bank and the payment system: to pay the loan out and collect an agreed payment.
  • NAMFISA, our auditor or accounting officer, the Namibia Revenue Agency, or a court: only where the law requires it.
  • Our attorney or debt collector: only if the account goes into serious default, and only after we’ve given you written notice.

We never sell your information. We never post about anyone’s debt online or in a WhatsApp group, and we never tell your family, colleagues or neighbours that you owe us money.

6. Where your information is kept

When you apply online, your details and documents go over an encrypted connection to our application service, which runs on Cloudflare. Documents are stored in private storage with no public links. Only our credit staff can open them, by signing in to our staff system, and every time staff open or change an application it is recorded.

Once a loan is granted, the loan account is kept in our loan book, which runs on Supabase and is reachable only by staff with their own login. These providers may store data outside Namibia. They act on our instructions only.

If we have reason to believe your information has been exposed, we will tell you as soon as we reasonably can, say what was affected and what we’re doing about it, and report it to NAMFISA.

7. How long we keep it

  • Applications you start but don’t hand in: deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Applications that are declined or withdrawn: documents deleted automatically after 180 days; a short record that the application was made is kept.
  • Loans: your agreement, quotation, affordability assessment, documents and payment records are kept for at least five years after the account is settled or closed, then deleted. This is our own policy; where the law requires longer, the longer period applies.

8. Your rights

  • To know what we hold about you, why, and who we give it to.
  • To see the information we hold about you: ask our Information Officer and we’ll respond within 10 business days, free of charge.
  • To have wrong information corrected, including at the credit bureau if we reported it.
  • To a free copy of your signed loan agreement, and a statement of your account.
  • To withdraw your consent. Withdrawal can’t undo what was already lawfully done, we must still keep the records in section 7, and while you owe us money we may still use your information to run and collect the account. If you withdraw before a loan is granted, we can’t grant it.
  • To complain, free of charge, first to us and then to NAMFISA.

9. Your consent when you apply

By ticking the boxes on the last sheet of the application, you agree that:

  1. We may obtain your credit report from a registered credit bureau, and report your account and payment behaviour to registered credit bureaux.
  2. We may contact your employer only to confirm your employment and that your payslip is genuine.
  3. We may contact you by WhatsApp, SMS, phone and email about your application and your account, privately and one to one.
  4. If your account falls into serious default, we may give your file to our attorney or debt collector, but only after first giving you written notice.

We don’t send marketing messages.

10. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice we’ll publish the new version here. We won’t apply a change that reduces your protection to information we already hold without asking you again.
